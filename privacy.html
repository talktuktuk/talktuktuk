Privacy Policy

Talk Tuk Tuk respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you use our language learning app.

1. Free access

You can use our 50 free video phrases and free quizzes without creating an account or providing your email address.

2. Premium purchases

Premium is available for a one-time payment of $4.99 USD. We use purchase references and recovery codes to verify and restore Premium access.

3. Payment information

Payments will be processed through our payment provider. Talk Tuk Tuk does not intend to collect or store your full payment card details.

4. Email addresses

Providing your email address is optional. If you agree to receive marketing emails, we may send you news, new phrases and app updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

5. Device registration

Premium access supports two registered devices or browser installations. We store a generated device identifier, device name and registration information to manage your access.

6. Recovery codes

Your recovery code allows you to restore Premium access. Keep it private and somewhere safe. Recovery codes are stored in hashed form on our server.

7. Offline learning

Downloaded videos, app information and access settings may be stored locally on your device to support offline learning.

8. Analytics and service providers

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the app. Our hosting, database and video storage providers may process technical information necessary to deliver the service.

9. Personal information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the app, process payments or comply with legal obligations.

10. Data retention and your rights

We retain information as necessary to provide Premium access, operate the service and meet legal obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Talk Tuk Tuk develops and introduces new features.
