Refund Policy

At Talk Tuk Tuk, we want every customer to enjoy their language learning experience.

1. Try before you buy

Talk Tuk Tuk provides 50 free video phrases and practice quizzes, allowing you to experience the app before purchasing Premium.

2. Premium purchases

Premium is a one-time payment of $4.99 USD for lifetime access to the purchased product. There are no subscriptions or recurring charges.

3. Refund requests

If you experience a problem with your Premium purchase, please contact us. We will review your request and aim to resolve the issue fairly.

Refunds may be available in circumstances including:

  • Duplicate or incorrect payments.
  • Payment completed but Premium access was not provided.
  • Technical problems that prevent access and cannot be resolved.
  • Other circumstances where a refund is required by applicable law.

4. How to request a refund

Email us with your purchase reference, approximate purchase date and a description of the problem.

Please do not send your full payment card details or recovery code.

5. Refund processing

Approved refunds will normally be returned through the original payment method. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider and your bank.

6. Premium access after a refund

Once a refund has been completed, your Premium licence will be cancelled. You can continue using the free version of Talk Tuk Tuk.

7. App store purchases

Purchases made through an app store may be subject to that store's refund procedures and policies.

8. Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits your statutory consumer rights under applicable law.
